# India Agriculture Problem Research — `EF-AGR`

This directory holds historical reports and syntheses on India-wide and global agricultural problem framing. It preserves research leads, context, causal models, and hypotheses that informed EcoFarm’s early thinking.

Most records are secondary synthesis or require revalidation. They must not be used as current, local, farmer-segment, or product-market-fit proof without attributable primary evidence and the appropriate scope.
