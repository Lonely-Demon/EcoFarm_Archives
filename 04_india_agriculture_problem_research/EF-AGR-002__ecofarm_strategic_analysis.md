## PART 2: WATER STRESS AFFECTING ECOFARM CUSTOMERS

### Groundwater Crisis in Customer Operating Regions

#### IGP (6 states, 500M people, customer base: 200M+ potential)
- **Crisis**: Groundwater depletion 50-100 cm/year
- **Timeline**: Critical 2030-2035
- **EcoFarm Solution**: Water-efficient crops, drip irrigation, mulching
- **Crops suited**: Pulses (gram, lentil), oilseeds (less water than wheat)
- **Yield impact without intervention**: 40-45% reduction by 2035

#### Deccan (4 states, 250M people, customer base: 100M+ potential, **PRIORITY**)
- **Crisis**: 8/12 years drought + crystalline aquifer depletion
- **Water table**: 1-3 m/year decline (very slow recovery)
- **Timeline**: Crystalline aquifer depletion very slow (permanent loss risk)
- **EcoFarm Solution**: Drought-tolerant crops, millets, pulses, water harvesting
- **Crops suited**: Bajra, jowar (native to region), pulses, oilseeds
- **Farmer impact**: Current situation → crop failure every 3-4 years
- **EcoFarm opportunity**: Alternative crop certification, market linkage

#### Western (2 states, 120M people, customer base: 50M+ potential)
- **Crisis**: 100-150 cm/year depletion + salinity
- **Additional challenge**: Desertification, livestock dependent
- **EcoFarm Solution**: Saline-tolerant crops, agroforestry, livestock integration
- **Opportunity**: Niche crops, livestock-crop integration

#### Coastal (8 states, 200M people, customer base: 80M+ potential)
- **Crisis**: Sea level rise 4.5cm by 2025, salinity intrusion 1-2 km/year
- **Unique challenge**: Permanent land loss (not recoverable)
- **EcoFarm Solution**: Salt-tolerant varieties (currently <1% coverage)
- **Opportunity**: Scaling salt-tolerant varieties from <1% to 20%+ (massive gap)
- **Impact**: 5-7M coastal farmers at risk of abandoning farming

#### Northeast (9 states, 100M people, customer base: 40M+ tribal farmers)
- **Crisis**: Shifting cultivation unsustainable + flooding
- **Unique**: Despite 2,000-3,500mm rainfall, flooding & waterlogging
- **Malnutrition**: 40-50% in tribal areas
- **EcoFarm Solution**: Jhum alternatives, flood-tolerant varieties, food security crops
- **Market gap**: Extension reach only 10-20% (massive opportunity)

## PART 5: CROP-SPECIFIC OPPORTUNITIES FOR ECOFARM

### High-Value Crop Market Gaps (Sustainable Focus)

#### Pulses (20% of cropped area, nutritious, water-efficient)
- **Current coverage**: Low in Deccan, Western regions
- **Water requirement**: 400-600mm (vs rice 1,200mm)
- **Market price**: ₹5,000-8,000 per quintal (premium for organic)
- **EcoFarm opportunity**: Organic pulse certification, aggregation
- **Customer appeal**: Deccan farmers (drought relief), Western farmers (water stress)

#### Millets (Ancient, water-efficient, high nutrition)
- **Current coverage**: <5% of area (declining)
- **Water requirement**: 300-400mm (lowest demand)
- **Market**: Emerging premium segment (health-conscious urban)
- **Price potential**: ₹3,000-6,000 per quintal (organic premium)
- **EcoFarm focus**: Deccan + Western regions, value-add (flour, flakes)
- **Customer appeal**: Severe drought areas, sustainability-focused

#### Oilseeds (Groundnut, sunflower, soybeans)
- **Current stress**: Soybean yield decline (Deccan pest issues)
- **Water efficiency**: Better than rice/wheat alternatives
- **Market**: Growing domestic + export demand
- **EcoFarm role**: Pest management, organic certification
- **Regional fit**: Deccan (soybean transition), Western (groundnut)

#### Vegetables (High-value, water-efficient with tech)
- **Current market**: 99% cold chain gap
- **Water tech**: Drip can reduce 40-50%
- **EcoFarm advantage**: Cold chain + precision irrigation
- **Crops**: Tomato, onion, potato (high volume), leafy greens (high margin)
- **Regional focus**: IGP (market proximity), Coastal (marine products)
