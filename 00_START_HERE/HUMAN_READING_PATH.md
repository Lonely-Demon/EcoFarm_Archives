# Human Reading Path

## A practical sequence

Begin with the [Master Knowledge Base](../10_knowledge_base_outputs/EcoFarm_Master_Knowledge_Base.md) for the project’s conceptual working, historical evolution, feature mechanics, technical reasoning, research landscape, unresolved alternatives, and evidence-aware context. It is the broadest human-readable synthesis, not a final specification.

Next, use the [HTML Knowledge Site](../10_knowledge_base_outputs/html_knowledge_site/index.html) when you want to move by topic rather than scroll through one large document. Its Evidence Atlas page is the correct place to understand authority, ambiguity, trace records, and revalidation gaps.

When a question concerns whether a statement is actually supported, open the [Evidence Atlas](../10_knowledge_base_outputs/evidence_atlas/EcoFarm_Evidence_Atlas.md) before treating any historical material as current. It distinguishes historical intent, product hypotheses, research designs, secondary syntheses, bounded evidence, and explicit unknowns.

Finally, use the [Curated Archive Index](../CURATED_ARCHIVE_INDEX.md) to reach the exact `EF-*` source behind a topic. The source file—not the index, a pitch, or a repeated statement—is the durable primary archive record.

## Topic routing

| Question type | Best first destination | Main source families to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| Why EcoFarm exists and how the vision changed | Master Knowledge Base, then Evidence Atlas | `EF-VSN`, `EF-GOV`, `EF-DSN` |
| How a proposed feature was meant to work | Product dossiers, then source index | `EF-PRD`, `EF-VSN` |
| Technology, sensors, AI, data, or architecture | Technical archive, then Atlas | `EF-TEC`, `EF-DAT` |
| India-wide or regional agricultural context | Research knowledge, then Atlas | `EF-AGR`, `EF-REG` |
| Farmer decisions and field-validation method | Field-validation archive, then Atlas | `EF-FLD`, `EF-DAT` |
| Market, ecosystem, or commercial hypotheses | Commercial archive, then Atlas | `EF-MKT`, `EF-FLD`, `EF-DAT` |
| What is unresolved or needs fresh verification | Evidence Atlas | `EVA-*` records and the linked `EF-*` files |

## Do not skip the evidence boundary

The archive intentionally retains ambitious ideas and contradictory historical material. A feature description is not evidence of implementation. A field guide is not a field outcome. A copied statistic is not current local proof. Where a present-tense external fact matters, inspect the Atlas revalidation records and then the relevant current primary source.
