| mKisan / Kisan Suvidha | India-wide government channels | Farmers receiving broadcast weather, market, and advisory information. | SMS/app; handset and registration required. | Official mKisan pages describe farmer SMS and Kisan Suvidha capabilities.[6] [7] | Existing information channel; not proof of local active use or interactive decision support. | Service documented; local reach verify. | Complement and benchmark. |
