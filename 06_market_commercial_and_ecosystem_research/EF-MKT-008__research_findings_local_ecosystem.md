# Verified research findings: local ecosystem

## Namakkal District Agriculture Department
Source: Government of Tamil Nadu, Namakkal District, Agriculture page. URL: https://namakkal.nic.in/departments/agriculture/ Accessed 2026-08-13.

Verified facts: The department describes district agricultural programmes including National Agriculture Development Programme, National Food Security Mission components, National Mission on Sustainable Agriculture, Soil Health Card Mission, bio-fertilizer/green-manuring, integrated nutrient and pest management, crop diversification, and value addition. It reports district area of 336,719 hectares, net cultivated area of 141,537 hectares, approximately 60,939 hectares irrigated and 80,598 hectares rainfed, Mettur East Bank Canal coverage of 4,585 hectares in Pallipalayam block, and normal annual rainfall of 716.54 mm. The page describes ATMA as strengthening research-extension-farmer linkages through demonstrations, training, and exposure visits. It documents a Bio-Control Laboratory at Namakkal and Soil Testing Laboratory at Namakkal, plus a mobile soil-testing laboratory at Tiruchengode. Soil samples are analyzed and soil-health cards generated/distributed.

Caution: The webpage documents departmental functions and infrastructure, not current utilization, response times, service-level availability, farmer adoption, or willingness to distribute EcoFarm. Current activity and local access require field verification.

Caution: The page carries a 2013 copyright and an Annual Report 2010-11 reference. Treat current service activity, staffing, farmer reach, digital delivery, and data-sharing as unknown until reconfirmed directly.

Initial product implication: EcoFarm should treat the department and KVK as validation/escalation/context channels, not as assumed partners or channels to replace. A narrow workflow should display source and timestamp, preserve links to original advisories, and route uncertain crop-risk cases to human/institutional escalation.
