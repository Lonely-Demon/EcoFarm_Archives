# Namakkal market and risk infrastructure notes

Source: Namakkal District Statistical Handbook 2023-24, official district administration PDF, access date 2026-08-13.

## Operational holding structure
The 2021-22 provisional Agricultural Census table reports 244,347 operational holdings covering 197,315.1 ha. Size classes include: up to 0.5 ha, 120,087 holdings; 0.5–1 ha, 53,265; 1–2 ha, 45,067; 2–3 ha, 14,340; 3–4 ha, 5,862; 4–5 ha, 2,738; 5–7.5 ha, 2,215; 7.5–10 ha, 481; 10–20 ha, 256; and 20 ha and above, 36. This is official descriptive evidence that the district has a very large number of small operational holdings. The table is provisional and the categories are holdings, not necessarily unique farm households.

## Regulated markets
The handbook lists six regulated market locations: Namagiripettai, Rasipuram, Solakkadu RM–Kollimalai, Namakkal, Tiruchengode, and Paramathi Velur. Crops listed across these markets include groundnut, maize, sorghum/jowar, coconut, turmeric, ragi, paddy, castor, sugarcane, black gram, horse gram, gingelly, and others. Reported total receipts for 2023-24 vary by market; the table's unit is lakhs but the exact receipt definition requires care. This confirms institutional market infrastructure exists; it does not demonstrate that small groundnut farmers can use it effectively or obtain better outcomes than through local traders.

## Crop insurance enrollment
For fasli year 1433 / 2023-24, the handbook reports 2,802 groundnut beneficiaries with insured amount Rs 63,881,717.62; 2,050 maize beneficiaries with Rs 65,907,470.94; 21,619 sorghum beneficiaries with Rs 191,899,931.5; 261 tapioca beneficiaries with Rs 10,991,769.64; and a district total of 29,743 beneficiaries with insured amount Rs 400,851,101.1. This indicates crop-risk management and insurance are materially relevant channels. It does not establish claim settlement quality, farmer awareness, or whether EcoFarm should address insurance.

Evidence label: Verified fact for handbook tables; supported inference only for implications about segmentation and market access. Confidence high for reported table contents, medium for generalization to current farmer behavior.
