# EcoFarm Alternatives Behavioural Verification Addendum

**Purpose.** Replace assumptions based on directories, app listings, and provider descriptions with observed measures of what farmers actually use around a sowing/early-establishment decision.

## Sampling frame

Recruit farmers across at least two target blocks and, if feasible, include rainfed and irrigated contexts, different farm sizes, and both digitally assisted and non-assisted users. Do not present the sample as representative of Namakkal. Record recruitment route and whether a local intermediary may influence responses.

## Behavioural interview instrument

| Measure | Question / observation | Record format |
|---|---|---|
| Last use | “When did you last use or speak to this channel?” | Date or “cannot remember”; channel. |
| Decision served | “What decision did it help you make?” | Sowing, seed, irrigation, pest, market, scheme, or other. |
| Specific action | “What did you do differently after receiving the information?” | Action changed, unchanged, or uncertain. |
| Response time | “How long did it take to receive a usable answer?” | Minutes/hours/days; include waiting and travel. |
| Trust reason | “Why did you trust or distrust it?” | Source authority, familiarity, accuracy history, peer proof, commercial relationship, or other. |
| Cost | “What did it cost in money, travel, phone/data, time, attention, or dependence on another person?” | Monetary and non-monetary estimate; do not infer willingness to pay. |
| Comprehension | Ask the farmer to explain the last message/advice in their own words. | Correct, partial, incorrect, or assisted. |
| Abandonment | “Have you stopped using it? Why?” | Too generic, unavailable, confusing, stale, costly, no action, commercial distrust, or other. |
| Conflict | “Did another person/source give different advice?” | Conflict source, resolution method, action taken. |
| Local relevance | “Did it match your crop, variety, stage, water, soil, and market?” | Match score plus explanation; avoid numerical confidence claims. |
| Human role | “Who helped you interpret or act on it?” | Family, dealer, trader, FPO, officer, KVK, peer, or nobody. |
| Switching | “What would make you use a new tool for this decision?” | Concrete trigger; not a hypothetical willingness-to-pay claim. |
| Outcome | “What happened after the action?” | Farmer-reported outcome and date; do not attribute causality. |

## Channel-specific verification

| Alternative | Behavioural evidence required before treating it as a competitor or complement | Minimum field threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Department / ATMA / KVK | Last contact, decision served, response time, whether advice was followed, and referral expectations. | At least repeated firsthand accounts across more than one block; otherwise classify as documented context only. |
| IMD/TNAU weather advisory | Actual receipt, interpretation, action change, forecast period understood, and conflict handling. | Evidence that farmers receive and act on it; otherwise retain as source context, not adoption claim. |
| Agmarknet/data.gov.in/eNAM | Whether farmer has seen the data, understands market/commodity/statistic, and uses it in a decision. | One confirmed local market/commodity use case plus semantic verification. |
| mKisan/Kisan Suvidha | Registration, last message/app use, language, comprehension, and action change. | Evidence of recent use in target segment; app availability alone is insufficient. |
| Private apps/input-linked platforms | App installed/used, last use, paid/free cost, agent contact, recommendation, commercial influence, and abandonment. | Direct user evidence; provider description alone is not local competition proof. |
| Dealers/traders/FPOs | Frequency of contact, advice type, commercial relationship, and whether the actor is trusted for the target decision. | Named actor and repeated user evidence; do not generalize from one interview. |
| Peer/WhatsApp/family | Message source, recency, language, verification method, and actual action. | At least one observed decision chain; classify reliability separately from popularity. |
| Status quo | Full decision reconstruction: sources consulted, sequence, time, conflict, final choice, and outcome. | Required for every pilot participant because it is the default alternative. |

## Behavioural measures for pilot comparison

The pilot should compare EcoFarm with the farmer’s existing process on: time to a decision; number of sources consulted; ability to identify source/date/limitation; ability to explain why the output applied; frequency of inappropriate confidence; referral acceptance; and whether the farmer changed or rejected the suggestion. A click, view, or registration is not evidence of useful adoption.

## Evidence discipline

Record direct quotes and observed actions separately from researcher interpretation. Tag every finding as verified observation, supported inference, product hypothesis, or unknown. Do not state that farmers “trust,” “prefer,” or “will pay” based on a hypothetical answer. Ask about the last real decision first, then test a prototype only after the baseline process is documented.
