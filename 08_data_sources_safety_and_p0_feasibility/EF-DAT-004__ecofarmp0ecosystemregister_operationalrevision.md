## Classification rule

A public contact may be shown as **current referral information** only after the URL, phone number, geography, and verification date are checked. A current referral is not a partner and carries no response-time promise. A **documented partner** requires a dated written agreement that names the relationship, permitted data/content use, referral responsibilities, and non-endorsement or endorsement language.
