# IMD weather-advisory evidence

Source: India Meteorological Department, Agromet Advisory Services, https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/agromet_adv_ser_state_current.php; access date 2026-08-13; primary official source.

Verified available channels: district-level weather forecast; state current, past English, and past local agromet bulletins; interactive map for agromet information; special agrimet advisories; KALP, described as location-specific weather prediction; SANKALP, an agrometeorological analytics, knowledge and advisory platform; forecasts and warnings at subdivision and district level.

Interpretation: Weather information is already an institutional service. EcoFarm cannot claim novelty merely by displaying forecasts. A defensible product hypothesis would need to test whether farmers need a more localized, crop-stage-specific translation into a bounded action, whether timing is adequate, whether advice is trusted, and whether following it changes an observable decision. Availability of an official service is verified; actual Namakkal farmer usage and satisfaction are unknown.
