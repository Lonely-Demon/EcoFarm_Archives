# Namakkal District Statistical Handbook 2023-24

Source: Namakkal District Administration, District Statistical Handbook 2023-24, published/listed 12 May 2025; official PDF URL: https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3b9228e0962a78b84f3d5d92f4faa000b/uploads/2025/05/2025051243.pdf; access date: 2026-08-13.

Extracted sections: rainfall tables; operational land holdings; crop area; irrigation.

Verified figures visible in extracted text:

- Operational holdings by size class: up to 0.5 ha = 120,087 holdings / 23,910.577 ha; 0.5–1.0 ha = 53,265 holdings / 38,485.345 ha; 1.0–2.0 ha = 45,067 holdings / 63,417.243 ha; 2.0–3.0 ha = 14,340 holdings / 34,595.878 ha; 3.0–4.0 ha = 5,862 holdings / 3,010.915 ha. The extracted section may continue beyond 4 ha; do not calculate a total distribution until all size classes are checked.

- Area irrigated by all sources (2023-24): total all crops 102,035.4 ha; groundnut 5,525.46 ha; tapioca 15,132.51 ha; jowar 16,013.885 ha; paddy 5,578.475 ha; onion 4,525.055 ha; sugarcrops 11,183.075 ha; total oilseeds 18,269.615 ha.

Interpretation: The official handbook supports a segmented design based on small operational holdings and irrigation status. It also shows that groundnut is not synonymous with the district crop system: irrigated area is substantial for jowar, tapioca, sugarcrops, vegetables, and other systems. This is a verified descriptive fact, not evidence of unmet demand or priority.

Caution: The handbook's crop and irrigation tables are official but the exact denominator and whether the crop table is net/gross or source-specific must be stated precisely from the table heading before using derived ratios. No derived percentages are included here.
