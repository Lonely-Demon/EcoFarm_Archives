## THE MSP TRAP (Why Punjab is Fundamentally Different)

### How MSP Blocks EcoFarm's Solution

```
Government MSP for wheat: ₹2,275/quintal (guaranteed)
↓
Farmer doesn't take price risk
↓
Alternative crop (bajra): No MSP, market price ₹1,200-1,500 (risky)
↓
Economic calculation: Wheat (guaranteed) > Bajra (risky)
↓
Farmer rationally chooses wheat despite water crisis
↓
EcoFarm recommends bajra (water-efficient)
↓
Farmer thinks: "App is asking me to lose ₹10K/hectare income"
↓
Farmer doesn't adopt
↓
EcoFarm MVP fails
```

### This is Structural: Can't be Solved by EcoFarm

- MSP is 70 years old (Deep institutional roots)
- Government won't change for 10-15 years minimum (Political economy)
- Farmer can't diversify alone (lacks ecosystem support)
- **EcoFarm's core value proposition (diversification) is economically irrational in Punjab**

**Punjab Reality**: Farmer **wants** to solve water crisis, but **can't** because government subsidy prevents rational economic decision.

## THE APMC TRAP (Why Punjab's Market Feature is Useless)

### How Arhtiya Debt Blocks EcoFarm's Market Linkage

```
Farmer owes arhtiya ₹100K (informal loan at 30% interest)
↓
Arhtiya: "Sell your wheat to me at mandi price. I'll deduct debt."
↓
Farmer has no choice (debt hostage situation)
↓
EcoFarm recommends: "Check 5 mandis; sell to highest price"
↓
Farmer can't (obligated to sell to arhtiya)
↓
EcoFarm's market feature is 100% useless
↓
Farmer abandons app
```

### This is Structural: Requires Credit System Reform

- Arhtiya system = 50+ years old
- Banks won't lend to small farmers (no collateral)
- Government won't reform for 10-15 years
- **EcoFarm's market linkage feature is economically impossible in Punjab (farmer can't access it)**

**Punjab Reality**: Even if EcoFarm shows farmer "sell here for 10% higher price," farmer **literally cannot** do it (debt prevents choice).
