# Research notes: local official source

## Namakkal District Agriculture Department
Source: Government of Tamil Nadu, Namakkal District, Agriculture page, https://namakkal.nic.in/departments/agriculture/; access date: 2026-08-13; source type: primary official.

Verified page statements: district geographical area 336,719 hectares; net cultivated area 141,537 hectares; approximately 60,939 hectares irrigated and 80,598 hectares rainfed; normal/average annual rainfall 716.54 mm, with rainfall noted as primarily North-East Monsoon; red soil reported as 77% of major soils; irrigation sources include canal, tank, tube well, lift irrigation and open well, with tube wells/bore wells contributing the major irrigated area; major crops listed are paddy, sorghum, groundnut, green gram, black gram, maize, cotton and sugarcane.

Interpretation: This supports a locally meaningful distinction between irrigated and rainfed farmer segments and confirms groundnut is one of several material crop systems. It does not establish which segment has the highest unmet decision need, farmer willingness to use/pay, or local product demand. The page's exact figures should be checked against the date/version of the official statistics before final publication.

Evidence label: Verified fact for page contents; supported inference for segmentation implications; confidence high for page statements, medium for currentness.
