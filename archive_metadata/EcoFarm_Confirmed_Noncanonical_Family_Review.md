## Non-negotiable archival distinction

The public curated archive now contains **only meaningful canonical materials, explicitly selected historical artefacts, metadata, and archive infrastructure**. The private preservation corpus retains raw duplicates, private conversation traces, workspace code, generated files, and other material that must remain available for audit but should never pollute ordinary EcoFarm evidence browsing.
