## 2. Revision Objective

Your first package identified **two tied leading candidates**: rainfed crop establishment/early-season response and in-season crop-risk response. The next product stage needs a disciplined way to distinguish them from real field evidence.

> **Your revision objective is to make the field programme capable of selecting one initial decision event—or explicitly refusing to select one—without allowing desk scores, founder familiarity, or interview enthusiasm to decide the outcome.**

The result should be a small set of operational documents that enables a 30–90 day field programme. It must not expand EcoFarm into a generic crop-advisory, market, or AI product study.

## 4. Evidence and Claim Requirements

All existing evidence rules remain in force. In addition, the fieldwork revision must distinguish the following states explicitly:

| State | What may be concluded |
| --- | --- |
| **Recalled event** | The farmer describes a past decision; useful for discovery but not direct outcome proof |
| **Observed live event** | A real decision trigger is witnessed or time-stamped; suitable for workflow testing |
| **Document-supported event** | A receipt, field note, weather bulletin, message, seed record, sale record, or other artifact corroborates part of the timeline |
| **Follow-up confirmed action** | The team has recontacted the participant after the decision window and recorded the actual action |
| **Attribution unresolved** | An action occurred, but EcoFarm’s contribution cannot be distinguished from other advice or constraints |

Do not call a field observation “validation” unless the decision, action, timing, and relevant comparison or counterfactual information are recorded. Do not treat the interview sample as representative of Namakkal or Tamil Nadu.
