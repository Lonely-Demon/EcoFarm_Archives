## 4. Required Data and Ecosystem Classifications

Every P0 source must be placed in one—and only one—of the following operational categories after testing:

| Classification | Meaning | Product consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approved P0 source** | Access, terms, freshness, geographic relevance, provenance, and failure behavior passed the required test | May support a constrained, source-labelled output |
| **Manual-reference source** | Reliable content but no automated/reuse right or inadequate operational access | May be linked or used by a human reviewer with attribution; not automated |
| **Context-only source** | Useful for research or background but not sufficiently local, timely, or actionable | Must not be used in a farmer decision packet |
| **Permission-dependent source** | May be valuable but needs farmer consent, owner permission, or institutional agreement | Must remain unavailable until permission is documented |
| **Rejected for the first pilot** | Cannot satisfy safety, relevance, or operational conditions | Must not be included in P0, regardless of technical appeal |

The revision must also classify every named institutional actor as **documented context**, **field-verification target**, **current referral information**, or **documented partner**. The last category may only be used with written evidence.

## 6. Completion Criteria

Your revision is complete only when a technical lead can answer the following without assuming access or inventing requirements:

1. **Which exact source(s) can be used for the P0 decision packet today, and what has been tested?**

1. **What data is shown as raw observation, what is transformed, and what may never become a predictive claim?**

1. **What happens if the source is stale, missing, contradictory, geographically too coarse, or inaccessible?**

1. **Which farmer data fields are strictly necessary, and what consent/retention/deletion process applies to each?**

1. **What referral can EcoFarm offer now without implying a partnership, response-time guarantee, or institutional endorsement?**

If a source cannot be fully tested from desk research, label it **verification required** and specify the precise owner, question, test artifact, and decision threshold. Do not upgrade it based on apparent credibility alone.
