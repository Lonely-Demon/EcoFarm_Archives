# EcoFarm Field Launch Kit — Governance Index

**Status:** Ready-to-deploy instrument package; no field validation has occurred in this workspace.  
**Objective:** Determine whether candidate A, candidate B, or N is supported by directly collected and coded evidence.  
**Cycle:** Day 1–7 launch and local-event confirmation, followed by a 30–90 day monitored programme.  
**Owner:** Field lead, with a separate safety reviewer and an evidence-coding reviewer where staffing permits.

## Non-negotiable governance

The team must not select a wedge, claim adoption, claim impact, recommend a product build, or label an event “validated” during the launch phase. Every event must carry an evidence state: recalled, planned, observed live, document-supported, follow-up confirmed, or attribution unresolved. Desk evidence is background only and cannot populate field-event counts.

## Data separation

The contact key must be stored separately from coded analysis records. The analysis record uses participant and event codes only. The contact key contains name, phone, safe contact route, consent status, and withdrawal request. Photos, messages, audio, and contact details are separate optional data categories; refusal must not exclude someone from non-media participation.

## Daily operating rhythm

At the start of each day, the field lead checks safe route, event windows, consent status, and staff safety. After each interaction, the interviewer completes the minimum event record before moving to the next participant. At the end of each day, the evidence lead checks missing states, authority fields, baseline action, other advice, follow-up date, and safety escalation. No retrospective memory should be silently rewritten as observation.

## Stop and escalate

Stop the interaction when consent is unclear, a participant is pressured, the request becomes pesticide/disease/yield/profit/buyer/price advice, personal information is requested unnecessarily, a staff member cannot maintain neutrality, or safe follow-up cannot be established. Escalate to the field lead and record the blocker. If local timing, recruitment independence, consent, or safe follow-up cannot be established by Day 7, the status memo must recommend retrospective discovery or pause rather than a live pilot.
