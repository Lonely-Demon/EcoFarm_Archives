# EcoFarm Seasonal Event and Observation Map

**Purpose.** Ensure the 30–90 day field programme observes real decision events where possible and does not pretend that a retrospective interview is live validation. This map is an operational planning hypothesis for **13 August–11 November 2026**. It is not a verified crop calendar. Before recruitment, the field lead must confirm village-specific windows with the farmer, KVK/Department of Agriculture, and at least one independent local informant.

> **Timing rule:** A date range in this document is a search window, not evidence that the event will occur. The field lead must record the source, date, village, crop, and confidence for every calendar update.

## 1. Event-state definitions

| State | Definition | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Recalled event | Farmer reconstructs a completed past decision | Useful for discovery, not direct outcome proof. |
| Planned event | Farmer expects a decision in a stated future window | Suitable for scheduling, not proof that it will occur. |
| Observed live event | Trigger, decision, and timing are witnessed or time-stamped during the window | Suitable for workflow testing, subject to corroboration. |
| Document-supported event | Receipt, field note, message, bulletin, seed record, sale record, or other artifact corroborates part of the timeline | Supports the corroborated fields only. |
| Follow-up confirmed action | Participant is recontacted after the decision window and reports/records what actually happened | Supports action measurement; attribution may remain unresolved. |
| Attribution unresolved | Action occurred but other advice, weather, cash, labour, or social constraints could also explain it | Do not claim EcoFarm influence. |

## 3. Field calendar by programme phase

| Programme period | Scheduling objective | Required actions | Minimum evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Confirm whether a live event is realistically imminent | Interview KVK/Department of Agriculture, two village informants, and initial farmers; record crop, village, actual sowing dates, and expected next decisions | Calendar confidence remains **unknown** until independent checks agree. |
| Days 8–14 | Complete discovery and install event diary | Conduct discovery interviews; ask each monitored farmer to report a trigger through the safest channel; capture baseline action before any EcoFarm workflow is shown | Recalled and planned events only unless a live trigger occurs. |
| Days 15–30 | Observe first decision window | Follow actual sow/wait/re-sow/early-establishment or crop-risk events; capture time-stamped baseline, other advice, action, and follow-up | Select only observed live or document-supported events for workflow-performance claims. |
| Days 31–60 | Extend follow-up or shift to the next crop-risk/sale window | Recontact after action; add comparative crop events; if no live event occurred, continue retrospective work but label it clearly | Action may be follow-up confirmed; attribution may remain unresolved. |
| Days 61–90 | Compare candidates and decide | Complete outcome follow-up, corroborate artifacts, apply the tie-break protocol, and decide narrow pilot / change / defer / stop | No wedge selection without minimum evidence thresholds. |

## 4. Event monitoring sheet

For every candidate event, record:

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Event ID | Unique code; no farmer name. |
| Village and crop system | Village, crop, variety if known, water status. |
| Expected event | Sowing, wait, re-sow, early inspection, pest/disease escalation, harvest, or sale. |
| Expected window | Start/end date, source of estimate, confidence. |
| Trigger | Rainfall, soil moisture, crop symptom, labour/input availability, cash need, buyer/price, or other. |
| Status | Recalled, planned, observed live, document-supported, follow-up confirmed, attribution unresolved. |
| Time stamps | Trigger first noticed; advice received; decision made; action executed; follow-up. |
| Evidence | Self-report, document, photograph, message, third-party observation, or unknown. |
| Next action | Interview, field visit, adviser review, receipt/photo capture, or follow-up call. |

## 5. If no live event is imminent

The field lead must not manufacture a live test. If no target crop is within a credible decision window, conduct a minimum retrospective design: **12 target-segment farmers**, each reconstructing one recent event; obtain at least **6 document-supported timelines** where possible; recontact all 12 after a scheduled next event; and observe at least **4 comparison-crop or alternative-decision events**. The result must be reported as discovery plus feasibility evidence, not workflow validation.

The product decision should be **deferred** if the programme cannot observe or follow up at least the minimum number of events specified in the tie-break protocol. A clean “not enough timing evidence” conclusion is preferable to an artificial wedge selection.

## 6. Local verification checklist

Before fieldwork begins, verify the following with source, date, and village: actual sowing dates for groundnut and comparison crops; whether farmers are sowing or re-sowing during the programme; early-establishment timing; locally relevant crop-risk events; expected harvest/sale windows; availability and timing of KVK/extension contact; typical rainfall/forecast information channels; and whether any event diary or follow-up contact is culturally and operationally feasible.
