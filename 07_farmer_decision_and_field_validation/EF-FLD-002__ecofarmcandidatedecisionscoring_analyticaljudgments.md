# EcoFarm candidate decision scoring — analytical judgments

Scoring scale: 1 = weak, 5 = strong. These are analyst judgments based on the desk evidence and product-safety criteria, not scientific measurements or farmer validation.

| Candidate decision problem | Frequency | Consequence | Urgency | Addressability | Trust burden | Data feasibility | Measurability | Field-access feasibility | Total / 40 | Desk assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Rainfed crop establishment and early-season response: whether/when to sow groundnut or switch/adjust after rainfall uncertainty | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 32 | Strongest initial wedge, but must be tested against actual sowing calendars, rainfall triggers, and farmers’ existing heuristics. |
| In-season crop-risk action: whether an observed pest/disease/soil-moisture signal warrants inspection, treatment, delay, or escalation | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 32 | Equally promising; more frequent and measurable, but misdiagnosis can cause material agronomic and financial harm. Requires expert escalation and no autonomous chemical recommendations in the first pilot. |
| Harvest/sale timing and market routing for groundnut/maize/tapioca | 2 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 30 | Valuable but constrained by liquidity, quality, storage, buyer relationships, and existing market channels. Test as transparent observation and comparison, not price prediction. |

Recommended desk hypothesis: begin with the first two as a paired field comparison, but select one only after observing the last crop cycle and the farmer’s real decision timeline. If a single wedge must be chosen before fieldwork, select **rainfed crop establishment and early-season response for small groundnut farmers**, with in-season risk response as the closest alternative.

Why not recommend a broad “AI farming app”: official agriculture, KVK, IMD, TNAU price, e-NAM, regulated markets, dealers, neighbors, and farmer experience already provide overlapping information. The unproven gap is not information absence; it is whether information is localized, timely, trusted, feasible, and translated into an observable action for a defined segment.
