# Farmer Decision and Field Validation — `EF-FLD`

This directory combines decision-model synthesis with the instruments required to generate attributable field evidence: research protocols, recruitment methods, consent material, interviews, seasonal planning, and follow-up records.

Field methods are not field results. Treat guides, maps, forms, and cohort designs as `research_design_only` unless recorded observations establish an outcome within a stated sample, location, and period.
