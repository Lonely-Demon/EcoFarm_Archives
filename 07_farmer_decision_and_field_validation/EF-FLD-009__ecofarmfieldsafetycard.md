# EcoFarm Field Safety Card

**Carry this card on every field interaction.** Research staff are not acting as agronomists, market agents, buyers, insurers, or government officers.

## Absolute boundaries

Staff must never give or imply:

- pesticide, fertilizer, disease, pest, weed, or treatment advice;
- yield, profit, crop-choice, or crop-outcome advice;
- buyer assurance, price advice, future-price prediction, or sale-timing advice;
- insurance, credit, legal, medical, or financial advice;
- a promise that KVK, the Department of Agriculture, IMD, a market, trader, buyer, or FPO will respond;
- a claim that EcoFarm can diagnose a field, guarantee weather, improve yield, or cause a financial result.

Do not recommend a brand, dose, chemical, crop switch, market route, storage decision, or price. Do not compare a participant’s practice with a “correct” practice during research. Do not use a participant’s question as permission to provide advice.

## Approved neutral referral wording

Use this wording, translated faithfully into the participant’s preferred language:

> “I am recording the question, but I am not qualified or authorized to answer it and I do not want to guess. Please contact the relevant public agriculture office, Krishi Vigyan Kendra, or other qualified local agricultural adviser through the public contact details you already use. I cannot promise that they will respond or that any outcome will follow. If the situation may cause immediate harm, please seek a qualified local professional promptly. We can record what source you contact and what happens, but we will not provide the recommendation.”

For market or price questions:

> “I cannot advise you on a price, buyer, sale timing, or profit. Please check the relevant public regulated-market or agricultural-marketing information source and a trusted local adviser. I cannot promise a buyer, a price, or a financial outcome.”

For urgent safety or health concerns related to inputs:

> “I cannot advise on chemical use or diagnose a crop problem. Do not rely on my guess. Please seek qualified local agricultural guidance and follow the applicable product label and safety requirements. I cannot promise the result.”

## Stop and escalate

Stop the interview and notify the field lead when: consent is unclear; anyone appears pressured; a participant asks for prohibited advice; a staff member is asked to inspect or diagnose a field; a participant requests a promise or transaction; an unsafe visit or follow-up route is identified; a commercial conflict appears; a staff member is unsure whether a statement is advice; or a participant reports immediate danger.

Record the event as a safety escalation, not as a product opportunity. Do not improvise a referral beyond the approved wording. The field lead may refer the participant to a public source using verified public contact information, but must not promise a response.

## Data and privacy rules

Never record phone numbers, names, or private messages in the coded event file. Do not photograph people, fields, receipts, or screens without separate permission. Do not forward participant messages to personal accounts. Do not audio-record without separate permission. Stop recording if the participant withdraws.
