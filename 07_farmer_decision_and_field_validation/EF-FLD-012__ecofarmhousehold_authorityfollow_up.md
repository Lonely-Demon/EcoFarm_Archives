## 3. Neutral verification questions

1. Who first noticed the issue? How do you know?
2. Who could have stopped the action?
3. Who decided what would happen next?
4. Who paid, borrowed, or authorized the spending?
5. Who physically performed or arranged the action?
6. Who received any farm message or advice?
7. Who explained or interpreted it?
8. If the outcome is checked later, who should report what actually happened?
9. Did any person disagree with the final plan? What happened?
10. Was the participant able to act independently, or did another person or constraint control the timing?

## 5. Safe follow-up permission

Ask separately: “May the research team contact you after the decision to record what actually happened?” Record yes/no. If yes, record the safe route in the separate contact key only. Do not put phone numbers in the coded event record.

If any person appears pressured, asks not to be contacted, or cannot safely participate, stop and record **follow-up not safe/feasible**. Do not attempt to persuade.
