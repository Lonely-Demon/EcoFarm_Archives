# EcoFarm Recruitment and Consent Pack

## 1. Recruitment script

“EcoFarm is conducting a short research study about how farm decisions are made in this area. We are not selling inputs, giving crop or market advice, promising a result, or asking you to change your practice. We may ask about one recent decision and, if you choose, contact you after a future event to record what happened. Participation is voluntary. You may refuse any question, refuse photos/messages/audio, decline follow-up, or stop at any time without losing any service or relationship.”

Do not describe a preferred outcome, mention the desk scores, or suggest that one candidate is expected to win. Do not recruit through a person who controls the participant’s access to essential services without explaining that refusal has no consequence.

## 3. Participant information and consent

Read or explain in the participant’s preferred language and check understanding:

- The purpose is to understand farm decision processes, not to provide advice.
- Participation may include a short discovery interview, a specific event record, a household-authority check, and a short post-event follow-up.
- There is no guaranteed benefit, payment, crop outcome, price, buyer, or service response.
- The participant may skip questions and may withdraw at any time.
- Research records will use a code. Contact details will be stored separately from coded analysis.
- Participation in one component does not require participation in another.
- Photos, screenshots/messages, audio recording, and follow-up contact each require separate permission.
- The team will not provide pesticide, disease, yield, profit, buyer, or price advice.
- Urgent or out-of-scope questions will receive only the neutral public-referral wording on the safety card.

## 4. Contact-key record — separate file

Store the following only in the protected contact key, never in the coded analysis table: participant code; name if voluntarily provided; phone number; safe contact method; safe time; whether a household member may see the contact; preferred language; facilitator route; consent dates; withdrawal date/status; and any safety note needed to avoid unwanted contact.

The coded analysis file stores only participant code, event code, village/field code, crop, event state, evidence class, authority role codes, action, follow-up status, confounders, and safety status.

## 5. Withdrawal route

Tell the participant: “You can stop now or later. You can say ‘stop’ to the interviewer, send a message or call the research contact, ask the facilitator to pass on the request, or tell any staff member. You do not need to give a reason. We will stop future contact and mark your code withdrawn. You may ask us to delete contact details and any optional photo, message, or audio that has not already been irreversibly anonymized or included in an approved aggregate analysis.”

Record withdrawal immediately in the contact key and mark the coded records as **withdrawn—do not contact**. Do not pressure the participant to continue or ask why they changed their mind.

## 6. Staff attestation

The interviewer confirms: I did not promise a benefit; did not give prohibited advice; explained separate permissions; recorded refusal without penalty; kept contact details separate; provided the withdrawal route; and stopped when consent or safety was unclear.
