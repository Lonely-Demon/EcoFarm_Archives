# EcoFarm Decision-Journey Tables

The tables below are desk-research hypotheses to be tested through event-based field observation. They are not completed farmer validation.

## Candidate 1: Rainfed crop establishment and early-season response

| Stage | Trigger and frequency | Decision owner and influencers | Current information/workaround | Action window | Consequence of weak decision | EcoFarm could credibly support | EcoFarm cannot solve alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-season crop choice | Each season; often before the monsoon or expected sowing window | Farmer or household head; family, experienced farmer, dealer, trader | Prior experience, neighbors, KVK/department, rainfall memory, input cost and market expectations | Days to weeks | Crop may not fit water, labour, or market conditions; opportunity cost | Compare a small number of feasible crops or crop timings using stated water, soil, labour, and uncertainty constraints | Guarantee rainfall, yield, profit, labour, credit, or buyers |
| Rainfall/sowing readiness | Once per sowing attempt, with repeat decisions if rain is delayed or planting fails | Farmer; family and labour availability; local advisers | Observed rain, soil moisture, local forecast, neighbor fields, dealer advice | Often a few days | Missed sowing window, re-sowing cost, poor stand, wasted seed/labour | Present date-specific observations, forecast uncertainty, and a conservative “wait / sow / inspect” choice set; record actual decision | Provide water, alter the weather, verify every field, or guarantee germination |
| Early stand establishment | First 1–3 weeks after sowing; triggered by poor emergence or dry spell | Farmer and field worker; adviser/dealer | Field inspection, own experience, neighbor comparison, input advice | Hours to days | Re-sowing, reduced plant population, avoidable input cost | Prompt inspection, capture crop stage and symptoms, recommend escalation where data are insufficient | Diagnose remotely with certainty or prescribe chemicals without expert confirmation |

## Candidate 2: In-season pest, disease, weed, or moisture-risk response

| Stage | Trigger and frequency | Decision owner and influencers | Current information/workaround | Action window | Consequence of weak decision | EcoFarm could credibly support | EcoFarm cannot solve alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Detect | Several times per crop cycle; triggered by symptom, weather change, or neighbor report | Farmer, family, hired worker | Visual inspection, neighbor, dealer, extension staff, WhatsApp/video content | Hours to several days | Spread, crop damage, unnecessary spraying, missed treatment window | Structured symptom capture, crop-stage prompt, local-language checklist, and expert escalation | Establish a diagnosis from a weak image or guarantee treatment efficacy |
| Decide action | Repeated whenever uncertainty remains | Farmer with dealer/extension/KVK input | Dealer recommendation, prior product use, label, local practice | Often same day | Cost, phytotoxicity, resistance, health/environmental harm, yield loss | Present “inspect again / seek expert / use already-approved practice” options with uncertainty and source | Issue high-stakes pesticide prescriptions, select a brand for commercial reasons, or replace agronomist judgment |
| Verify result | Days after action | Farmer and adviser | Field observation, repeat spray, neighbor comparison | Days | Repeated cost or delayed escalation | Ask for outcome record and flag non-response for human review | Independently verify causality or recover lost yield |

## Candidate 3: Harvest, storage, and sale timing

| Stage | Trigger and frequency | Decision owner and influencers | Current information/workaround | Action window | Consequence of weak decision | EcoFarm could credibly support | EcoFarm cannot solve alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assess harvest readiness | Once per crop cycle, with quality and weather triggers | Farmer; labour, buyer, trader, family cash needs | Crop maturity signs, trader inspection, neighbor practice, weather forecast | Days | Quality deterioration, harvest loss, poor timing | Record maturity cues, local weather, and alternatives | Determine maturity remotely or guarantee quality |
| Choose sale route | Once or in batches | Farmer; trader, FPO, regulated market, household debt/cash needs | Dealer/trader quote, local market, regulated market, e-NAM/AGMARKNET observation | Hours to days | Lower price realization, transport or grading loss, delayed cash | Transparent comparison of observed prices, quality assumptions, transport and timing; record final transaction | Predict price with certainty, create a buyer, provide storage/credit, or remove bargaining asymmetry |
| Decide whether to delay | Only if storage, cash, and quality allow | Farmer and household decision-makers | Debt pressure, storage access, trader relationship, price expectation | Days to weeks | Spoilage, price decline, or inability to meet cash needs | Make constraints visible and log the decision | Change liquidity, storage, buyer terms, or household obligations |

## Cross-candidate measurement model

| Measurement | Candidate 1 | Candidate 2 | Candidate 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision event captured | Sowing or re-sowing date and stated reason | Symptom detected, advice sought, action taken | Harvest/sale date, route, quantity and quality basis |
| Pre-action confidence | 0–10 farmer confidence, recorded before action | 0–10 confidence in identification/action | 0–10 confidence in sale timing/route |
| Timeliness | Days between trigger and decision | Hours/days between symptom and escalation | Hours/days between harvest readiness and sale |
| Observable behavior | Sowing, waiting, switching, or inspection | Inspection, escalation, treatment, or no action | Quote comparison, route choice, delayed sale, final receipt |
| Outcome | Establishment, re-sowing, crop stage, cost | Symptom progression, repeat action, cost | Realized price, deductions, transport, delay, quality |
| Safety failure | Advice issued despite missing rainfall, soil, or crop-stage facts | Misdiagnosis or unsafe input recommendation | Overconfident price prediction or implied financial guarantee |
