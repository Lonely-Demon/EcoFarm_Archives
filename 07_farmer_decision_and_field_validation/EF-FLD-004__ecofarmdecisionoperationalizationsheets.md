# EcoFarm Decision Operationalization Sheets

These sheets convert broad candidate workflows into observable sub-decisions. They define what EcoFarm may test, what it must refuse, and what outcome must be followed up. They are hypotheses until a local agronomist and monitored farmers confirm the event timing.

## Candidate A: Rainfed sowing and early-establishment response

### A1. Decide whether to sow after a rainfall or moisture signal

| Field | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Farmer receives or observes rainfall, soil-moisture change, forecast, or local field comparison that could make sowing feasible. |
| Decision owner | Person who controls the sowing decision; record initiator, influencer, payer, veto holder, and executor separately. |
| Time window | Target window is the farmer-stated period between trigger and feasible sowing; record hours/days rather than imposing a fixed threshold. |
| Minimum inputs | Village/field; crop and variety if known; intended acreage; recent rainfall or soil observation; water status; seed availability; labour/equipment availability; expected weather source and timestamp; constraints that make an option infeasible. |
| Feasible actions | Sow; wait and inspect at a specified time; reduce/adjust intended area; choose a pre-existing alternative crop; seek human agronomic review; do nothing because constraints make action infeasible. |
| Data source | Farmer observation; time-stamped message/bulletin; field observation; local adviser; KVK/Department of Agriculture; IMD source where relevant. |
| Refusal conditions | Missing location or crop; no reliable timing; no information on water or soil condition; farmer asks for guaranteed yield/profit; output would require unsupported crop-specific prescription. |
| Escalation condition | Conflicting sources; unusual weather; uncertain seed or soil status; financially material switch; request for chemical or disease advice; decision outside validated crop and geography. |
| Observed action | Actual sow/wait/adjust/seek-advice/non-action, with timestamp, actor, and stated reason. |
| Follow-up outcome | Establishment observation; re-sowing; acreage changed; seed/labour/input cost; crop-stage status; farmer’s post-action confidence; other constraints or events. |

### A2. Decide whether to re-sow or adjust after poor emergence

| Field | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Farmer observes delayed, uneven, or poor emergence, or reports a weather/soil event after sowing. |
| Decision owner | Record who authorizes additional seed/labour/cost and who executes the field action. |
| Time window | Farmer-stated feasible period before re-sowing becomes impractical; record actual first observation and decision time. |
| Minimum inputs | Sowing date; crop stage; affected area; field observation; recent rainfall/irrigation; seed and labour availability; prior action; adviser inputs. |
| Feasible actions | Inspect again; consult agronomist/KVK/extension; re-sow affected area; adjust area; continue monitoring; switch only if a locally feasible and already-understood alternative exists. |
| Data source | Field visit or farmer time-stamped observation; seed receipt/field note where available; adviser review. |
| Refusal conditions | No field observation or credible description; request for guaranteed recovery; recommendation would require diagnosing seed, soil, pest, or disease cause without adequate evidence. |
| Escalation condition | Large affected area; repeated failure; suspected disease/pest; high-cost re-sowing; disagreement among advisers. |
| Observed action | Re-sow, wait, inspect, adjust, or escalate; record who acted and when. |
| Follow-up outcome | Emergence after action; additional cost; affected acreage; repeat action; crop-stage consequence; unresolved cause. |

### A3. Decide whether to change crop or abandon the intended sowing plan

| Field | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Sowing window or water condition is judged unsuitable, or seed/labour/cash constraints change. |
| Decision owner | Household authority map is mandatory; record who can veto or fund the change. |
| Time window | Farmer-stated deadline after which the intended crop is no longer feasible. |
| Minimum inputs | Intended crop; alternative already considered by farmer; water; labour; seed; cash; market route; agronomic fit; household need. |
| Feasible actions | Continue intended plan; wait; reduce acreage; use a previously considered alternative; leave fallow; seek human review. |
| Data source | Farmer’s own records, local adviser, official agronomic source, observed field conditions. |
| Refusal conditions | EcoFarm lacks local crop/season evidence, cannot compare water/labour/cash feasibility, or would present a profit ranking. |
| Escalation condition | Major crop-switch decision, debt or food-security consequence, or disagreement among household decision-makers. |
| Observed action | Actual crop plan and execution, not merely stated preference. |
| Follow-up outcome | Crop established, plan changed again, cost incurred, household consequence, and farmer’s retrospective explanation. |

## Candidate B: In-season crop-risk response

### B1. Decide whether a field symptom requires inspection or escalation

| Field | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Farmer notices a symptom, pest, weed, nutrient concern, moisture issue, or neighbor warning. |
| Decision owner | Person who first detects, decides, pays, and executes; these may differ. |
| Time window | Time from first observation to inspection/escalation; record actual hours/days. |
| Minimum inputs | Crop, variety if known, stage, location; symptom onset; affected area; weather and irrigation; photograph or field observation; recent input; nearby field context. |
| Feasible actions | Inspect; monitor; seek KVK/extension/agronomist review; compare with a trusted local source; take no action pending evidence. |
| Data source | Field observation, farmer image, adviser, KVK/Department, official crop guidance. |
| Refusal conditions | Image/description insufficient; crop or stage unknown; request for chemical treatment; symptom could have multiple high-consequence causes. |
| Escalation condition | Rapid spread, large affected area, suspected disease/pest, chemical request, conflicting diagnoses, or safety concern. |
| Observed action | Inspection, escalation, treatment by another qualified source, monitoring, or no action. |
| Follow-up outcome | Expert-confirmed or unresolved cause, symptom progression, cost, repeat treatment, crop response. |

### B2. Decide whether to take a treatment action

| Field | Operational definition |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Qualified adviser or existing approved practice gives a treatment option after adequate inspection. |
| Decision owner | Person approving purchase and application; record payer and executor. |
| Time window | Stated by the qualified source; EcoFarm must not invent a treatment deadline. |
| Minimum inputs | Qualified source; crop/stage; confirmed or sufficiently bounded problem; label/regulatory context; existing product details; protective and application constraints. |
| Feasible actions | Follow qualified advice; seek second opinion; monitor; use a non-chemical cultural action if explicitly supported; do not act pending review. |
| Data source | Qualified agronomist/KVK/Department/label; farmer observation. |
| Refusal conditions | No qualified source, no sufficient diagnosis, product/dose unknown, commercial conflict undisclosed, or request for autonomous prescription. |
| Escalation condition | Any high-risk chemical question, severe spread, food-safety concern, or disagreement. |
| Observed action | Treatment/monitoring/escalation/non-action, recorded independently of EcoFarm exposure. |
| Follow-up outcome | Symptom response, repeat action, cost, adverse effect, and expert assessment where possible. |

## Candidate tie-break measurement fields

For both candidates, capture the same fields so comparison is possible: number of eligible events per farmer; number observed live; trigger-to-decision time; number of independent advice sources; baseline action before EcoFarm exposure; actual action; reason for action; feasibility constraints; outcome observed; follow-up completion; safety escalations; and whether attribution is resolved.

No candidate may be selected solely because it generates more conversation, receives higher stated interest, or is easier for the founder to access.
