# Namakkal rainfed groundnut study

Source: Jayashree, K.V. and Saravanan, K. (2026), “An Empirical Study on Groundnut Cultivation Practices in Rainfed Zones of Namakkal District, Tamil Nadu,” South India Journal of Social Sciences 24(2), pp. 142–147, DOI https://doi.org/10.62656/SIJSS.v24i2.2297; article page https://journal.sijss.com/index.php/home/article/view/2297; accessed 2026-08-13.

The article states that its primary data were collected from 120 farmers using random sampling. It examines factors influencing cultivation practices, cost structure, sowing schedules, and value-added practice adoption. The abstract reports a significant association between farm size and sowing season, a strong positive correlation between land area and production volume, and that land-preparation cost was the most influential factor affecting operating costs in its regression analysis. It argues for better price realization, training in value addition, and policy support.

Evidence use: This is direct first-party field evidence from a published study, but it is not EcoFarm’s own validation. The article’s sample frame, villages/blocks, questionnaire, and representativeness must be checked in the full PDF before making granular local claims. It supports testing sowing-timing and cost-management decisions among rainfed groundnut farmers; it does not prove that weather advice, price information, or an app is the highest-value product wedge.

Evidence label: Verified fact for the article’s stated methods and abstract findings; supported inference for product implications; confidence medium because only the abstract/page was reviewed.
