# EcoFarm Short Post-Event Follow-Up

**Target duration:** 5–10 minutes. Complete after the decision window, not immediately after exposure. This form records what happened; it does not ask the participant to rate EcoFarm before reconstructing the event.

## 2. Actual action

1. What did you actually do after the earlier decision point?
2. When did you do it?
3. Who decided, paid, and executed it?
4. Did the action differ from the baseline plan stated earlier? How?
5. What other advice, weather, household discussion, cash change, labour change, or market event occurred before the action?

Ask only for outcomes relevant to the event. Do not request a yield, profit, or price estimate if the participant cannot provide it safely and clearly. Never infer causality from a good or bad outcome.

## 4. Confidence and usefulness

1. How confident are you now in the decision, from 0–10?
2. What remains uncertain?
3. What source or person would you consult next?
4. Did the study interaction change anything? Ask only after the neutral questions above.

Code the last answer as a perception, not an impact measure.
