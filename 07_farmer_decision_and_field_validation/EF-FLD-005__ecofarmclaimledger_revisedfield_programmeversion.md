## Event-state vocabulary for all future claims

| State | What may be concluded |
|---|---|
| **Recalled event** | The farmer described a past decision; useful for discovery, not direct outcome proof. |
| **Observed live event** | A real trigger and decision were witnessed or time-stamped; suitable for workflow testing. |
| **Document-supported event** | An artifact corroborated part of the timeline; use only for corroborated fields. |
| **Follow-up confirmed action** | The actual action was recorded after the decision window; outcome and attribution still require separate coding. |
| **Attribution unresolved** | An action occurred, but other advice or constraints could explain it; do not claim EcoFarm caused it. |

No field observation should be labelled **validation** unless the decision, action, timing, relevant comparison or baseline, and follow-up status are recorded. The interview sample remains non-representative.
