# EcoFarm Field Selection Memo

## 1. What is ready to test now

EcoFarm is ready to test a **human-supervised decision-reconstruction and follow-up workflow** with two competing candidates: **A. rainfed sow / wait / re-sow / early-establishment response**, and **B. in-season crop-risk triage and escalation**. The provisional target is small, primarily rainfed Namakkal farmers growing groundnut or a comparable dryland crop, with a required comparison cohort covering non-groundnut crops and different water/advisory situations.

The field programme is ready to begin discovery with 12 farmers, a minimum primary monitored cohort of 12 farmers, an 8-farmer comparison-crop cohort, and six adviser/market interviews. The programme has a 90-day scheduling map, decision operationalization sheets, household authority matrix, counterfactual questions, evidence-quality classes, follow-up fields, safety refusal conditions, and a pre-specified tie-break protocol. Exact village event dates are **not** yet ready to be treated as facts; they must be confirmed locally before recruitment.

The current test must record: the trigger; baseline action without EcoFarm; all other advice sources; initiator, decision-maker, payer, veto holder, executor, phone owner, interpreter, and follow-up authority; EcoFarm exposure and timing if any; actual action; constraint; evidence class; follow-up outcome; and attribution status. It may measure recurrence, time to decision, confidence change, action completion, escalation, and outcome observability. It must not promise or test guaranteed profit, future price, yield, buyer access, insurance settlement, autonomous pesticide/disease treatment, hardware actuation, or a generic agricultural operating system.

## 2. What remains an unknown that Product, UI/UX, and Development must not assume

The desk evidence does not establish that a live sowing or crop-risk event will occur during the next 30 days, that groundnut is the strongest crop wedge, or that either candidate is primarily an information problem rather than a water, labour, cash, credit, storage, input, or buyer constraint. It does not establish who holds household authority, who controls the phone, who pays, who executes the work, or whose advice is trusted in the target villages.

The team must not assume smartphone ownership, independent phone access, literacy, Tamil-language preference, direct receipt of information, willingness to share farm data, willingness to pay, trust in AI, use of KVK/IMD/e-NAM, or the ability to act on advice within the relevant time window. A farmer’s interest, stated intention, or action after a conversation does not establish adoption, impact, or causality. A forecast, image, market quote, or crop label is insufficient for safe recommendations without location, timing, crop stage, constraints, source, and uncertainty. A short field programme cannot establish yield, profit, price improvement, or causal impact without stronger data and comparison.

## 3. What specific field evidence will trigger the decision to start a narrow pilot build

Start a narrow pilot build only when one candidate satisfies every hard gate in the tie-break protocol: at least eight target-segment farmers monitored, at least six independent of founder/family networks; at least six eligible events reaching follow-up-confirmed action; at least four live or document-supported events; events in at least two independent field contexts; household authority complete for at least 80% of included events; baseline action, other advice, EcoFarm exposure timing, and reason for action or rejection captured for at least 80%; no unresolved safety pathway requiring autonomous agronomic or market advice; and an outcome observable within 90 days or explicitly labelled as an interim proxy.

After the gates, select sowing/early-establishment over crop-risk response only if it scores at least five points higher on the pre-specified 45-point matrix and has no score below 3 for workaround gap, actionability, data sufficiency, or safety. Select crop-risk response under the same rule if it wins. Elevate a comparison crop only if it is more recurrent, actionable, safer, and better measured by at least five points. If the lead is less than five points, if no candidate reaches the gates, or if the binding constraint is not information-addressable, **do not build**: extend fieldwork, change the segment, or defer software. Any build must preserve human escalation, uncertainty disclosure, source visibility, assisted/local-language use, and the prohibition on guarantees, autonomous pesticide/disease prescriptions, future-price predictions, buyer assurance, hardware actuation, and generic agricultural operating-system scope.
